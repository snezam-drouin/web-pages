Canadian Transportation Agency

The Canadian Transportation Agency is an independent, quasi-judicial tribunal and economic regulator. It makes decisions and determinations on a wide range of matters concerning air, rail and marine modes of transportation under the authority of Parliament, in accordance with the Canada Transportation Act and other legislation.

Compliance monitoring and enforcement

The Canadian Transportation Agency is an independent regulator and quasi-judicial tribunal that uses a variety of tools to verify the compliance of 1,500 service providers in the air, rail, marine and interprovincial bus sectors.

Consultations

The Canadian Transportation Agency is committed to consulting with Canadians. Through consultations, the Agency gains a greater understanding of different perspectives from the public, stakeholders and experts and therefore develops more informed and effective policies and programs.

Air passenger protection

You have rights when you travel by air. The CTA has developed the Air Passenger Protection Regulations (APPR) to establish the minimum obligations of airlines towards passengers.
